About the project

SafetyPulse

An IoT-based wristband with AI fall and heat-related strain detection for RL Construction and Supply Corporation. It detects falls and monitors signs of heat strain in construction workers using motion, impact, body temperature and heart rate sensors.

A barometer and Bluetooth Low Energy beacons locate the worker's floor and area. Decision tree and random forest models analyze the data, and hazard alerts are sent over BLE 5.0 to a mobile app for supervisors and safety officers.
